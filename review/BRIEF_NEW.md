# Review of the NEW levels (Oct 2026) — extra rules on top of review/BRIEF.md

Read `review/BRIEF.md` (the general review brief — follow all of it) and `content/CURRICULUM_NEW_LEVELS.md`
(unit table, sizes, `pr` conventions). These units were just written by two different authors per level; you are the
independent expert checker. Correctness, rigour and pronunciation are the top priorities.

Additional checks for the new levels:
1. **Level fit and progression**: each unit teaches its curriculum grammar correctly and completely, at the right CEFR level;
   no grammar from a higher level used as if known (fixed phrases are OK); nothing that the level below already taught presented as new.
   Compare with the neighbouring levels' files (e.g. for German A2 look at de-en/A1 and de-en/B2 units).
2. **Exercise `pr`**: must be the FULL correct sentence (gap filled with the right answer, no "___"), because the app shows it
   after the learner answers. Convert any `pr` that still contains ___.
3. **`pr` conventions** exactly as in CURRICULUM_NEW_LEVELS.md, identical in both halves of the level. English: "your" = «یور»,
   /ɔɪ/ = ُی (point «پُینت»). Where no rule exists, follow the majority usage of the existing levels of the same course and make
   both halves of your level identical. English names by sound is fine, Persian names in normal Persian spelling is fine — but be
   consistent within the level.
4. **Factual claims** in readings and examples must be true and stable over time. If a fact is doubtful or likely to change
   (laws, statistics, "the oldest …"), make it clearly general or hedge it correctly; never leave a possibly false fact.
5. **Duplicates**: no headword used twice inside the level; avoid headwords already taught in other levels of the same course
   (replace with a fitting new word if you find one — keep ids).
6. **`alts`** on order items: every alternative must be a correct, natural sentence made of exactly the same words; add missing
   ones where another natural order exists.
7. The app shuffles multiple-choice options, so answer positions do not matter.

Report: `review/<course>-<LEVEL>-new.md` with the same structure as BRIEF.md asks. Final message: number of changes per
category and the most important fixes.
