# Zabanyar A1 curriculum (same themes in all three courses; grammar adapted per language)

Learners: an Iranian family (adults, some older, some total beginners). Warm, encouraging tone.
Units build on each other; only use language from the current and earlier units (plus obvious names/numbers).

| Unit | Theme | en-fa (English, Persian support) | de-en (German, English support) | tr-fa (Turkish, Persian support) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| u00 | Alphabet & sounds | 26 letters, letter names, key sounds (th, sh, ch, silent e), simple words | 26 letters + ä ö ü ß; sounds ei/ie/eu/ch/sch/sp/st/z/w/v | 29 letters (ç ğ ı ö ş ü), "every letter is pronounced", vowel harmony intro (e-type / a-type vowels) |
| u01 | Hello! Greetings & introductions | to be: I am / you are / he-she is; subject pronouns; What's your name? Where are you from? | sein (ich bin, du bist, er/sie ist, Sie sind); ich heiße; du vs Sie; Woher kommen Sie? | personal endings for "to be" (-(y)ım, -sın, -dır…) with vowel harmony; Adım …; Nerelisin? |
| u02 | Numbers 0–20 & age | numbers; How old are you? I'm … years old; plural -s | Zahlen 0–20; Wie alt bist du? Ich bin … Jahre alt; regular verb endings ich/du/er (wohnen, kommen) | sayılar 0–20; Kaç yaşındasın? … yaşındayım; plural -ler/-lar |
| u03 | My family | possessive adjectives my/your/his/her/our; 's (Sara's brother); have got / have | Familie; der/die/das; possessives mein/meine, dein/deine; haben | aile; possessive suffixes (annem, annen, annesi); benim/senin |
| u04 | Food & drink | a / an; some; I like / I don't like; I'd like … please | Essen & Trinken; ein/eine/kein/keine; ich möchte; mögen (ich mag) | yiyecek-içecek; istiyorum / istemiyorum; seviyorum; "… lütfen"; çay, ekmek… |
| u05 | My home | there is / there are; in / on / under / next to | Wohnung; es gibt + Akkusativ (einen/eine/ein); Wo ist …? | ev; var / yok; locative -de/-da (-te/-ta); nerede? |
| u06 | My day & time | present simple I/you/he-she (get up, works, -s); What time is it? It's seven o'clock | Tagesablauf; Uhrzeit (Es ist sieben Uhr / halb acht); separable verbs (aufstehen); verb second word order | günlük hayat; present continuous -(i)yor (kalkıyorum, çalışıyor); Saat kaç? Saat yedi |
| u07 | In the city | places; Where is …? imperatives (go straight, turn left); prepositions near / opposite | Stadt; Wo ist …? Imperativ (Sie): Gehen Sie geradeaus; links/rechts; in der / im | şehir; … nerede?; dative -(y)e/-(y)a (okula gidiyorum); sağa/sola dönün, düz gidin |
| u08 | Shopping, clothes & colours | this / that / these / those; How much is it?; colours before nouns | Einkaufen; Kleidung, Farben; Wie viel kostet …?; Akkusativ (den/einen); dieser/diese | alışveriş; renkler; Kaç lira?; bu / şu / o; Bunu alabilir miyim? |
| u09 | Weather, months & seasons | It's sunny / it's raining; months; seasons; in + month | Wetter; Es ist sonnig / es regnet; Monate, Jahreszeiten; im Juli, im Sommer | hava; Hava güneşli / yağmur yağıyor; aylar, mevsimler; Temmuzda, yazın |
| u10 | Free time & hobbies | can / can't; like + -ing; Do you …? questions | Freizeit; können (ich kann schwimmen) + verb at end; gern; Ja/Nein-Fragen | boş zaman; -ebilmek (yüzebilirim / yüzemem); -mek sevmek (okumayı severim); soru eki mi? |

Levels after A1 (outline only for now — shown on the map as "coming soon"):
- A2 — past tense, future plans, travel, health, work, comparisons
- B1 — experiences, opinions, stories, news, longer texts
- B2 — debates, abstract topics, complex grammar, authentic texts
- C1 — fluency, idioms, style, professional language
