# tr-fa cast sheet (Turkish for Persian speakers, A1–C1)

One family, one set of friends, the same facts in every level. Before you write a dialogue, reading or example that says
something about a named person (job, age, family, city), check it here. One-off service roles (waiter, seller, doctor,
receptionist, clerk, repair man, TV host, journalist, bookseller…) do **not** get a cast name: use a role label as the
speaker name (`Garson`, `Satıcı`, `Doktor`, `Gişe görevlisi`, `Resepsiyonist`, `Satış danışmanı`, `Tamirci`, `Görevli`,
`Muhabir`, `Sunucu`, `Sahaf`, `Gönüllü`, a passer-by `Adam` / `Kadın`) or a side name from the list below.
Plain grammar examples ("Ali kahve istemiyor.", "Elif sinemaya gelmeyecek mi?") may use any cast name as long as they do not
contradict a fact below. Never use "Neda".

**Time line.** Ages are given **at A1**. Each level is roughly one year later (Mina is 32 in B1, Sara applies for a
master's in B2, Ayşe is a lawyer in C1). The Karimis moved from Tahran to İstanbul two years before A1 (B2 u06 "altı yıl önce").
