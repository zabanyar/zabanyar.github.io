# de-en cast sheet (all levels A1–C1)

The German course follows one Iranian family in Köln and the Germans around them. Every dialogue, reading, example and
exercise must agree with this sheet. Before you write a story about a cast member, check their facts here; if a story
needs a different age, job, city or family, use a **side character** instead (a new name, see the end).

A name in a one-line grammar example needs no biography, but it must not contradict a hard fact below
(children stay children, married people stay married to the same person, nobody moves to another city).
Never use the name **Neda**.

## The Ahmadi family (Köln)

They came from Isfahan to Köln about six years ago (A2 says "seit fünf Jahren", B2 "seit sechs Jahren").
They moved once inside Köln, into a bigger five-room apartment with a balcony on the *zweiter Stock* of the Wagners'
building (A2 u03, B1 u03 and B2 u02 show the same move). They have no TV and a cat named **Mimi**.

| Name | Age | Facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Ali Ahmadi** | about 45 | Father, Maryam's husband. Drinks coffee, plays soccer. Took the Integrationskurs; now helps newcomers with forms. Travels by train for work. His parents and a brother live in Tehran. Reza is his cousin. |
| **Maryam Ahmadi** ("Frau Ahmadi") | about 42 | Mother, Ali's wife. Office job, part-time (colleague: Jonas). Drinks tea, reads, sings in a choir (B2). Her sister lives in Tehran; her mother moved from Tehran into the family's apartment two years ago (B1 u08). |
| **Mina** | 12 | Daughter. School; reads, sings, swims, cooks. Friend of Lukas. |
| **Amir** | 10 | Son. Can't swim, but cooks well. |

## Reza and Sara (Ali's cousins, Köln)

Reza and Sara are siblings. Their parents also live in Köln now (married 45 years, B1 u08); their grandmother is alive.

| Name | Age | Facts |
