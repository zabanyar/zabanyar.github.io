# Zabanyar — English A2 & B1 (course en-fa) and Turkish B1 & B2 (course tr-fa)

Learners: an Iranian family (adults, mixed ages). Support language for BOTH courses: **Persian** (Iranian Farsi) — all
`s` translations, `goals`, grammar `explain` texts in natural Persian with proper ZWNJ half-spaces and Persian ی/ک.
Same JSON schema as A1 (`content/SCHEMA.md`) with these level adjustments (they override the A1 sizes):

- Files: `content/en-fa/A2/u01…u10.json`, `content/en-fa/B1/u01…u10.json`, `content/tr-fa/B1/u01…u10.json`, `content/tr-fa/B2/u01…u10.json`;
  set `"level"` accordingly. No u00 at these levels.
- `words`: 12–16 topic words/collocations at the right level. English: American spelling. `pr` (pronunciation in Persian script):
  give it on every word at A2 and B1 (still helpful for new words); for Turkish B1/B2 give `pr` on words only.
  `emoji` only if one standard emoji clearly pictures the word, else "".
- `phrases`: 6–8 useful chunks (functional language: suggesting, agreeing, complaining, giving opinions…), with `pr` at en A2/B1.
- `dialogue`: 8–12 lines, two adult speakers (f/m), realistic situations.
- `grammar`: exactly 2 points; Persian explanation (clear, precise, **bold** forms, compare with Persian where it really helps),
  optional table, 4–5 examples, 6–8 exercises (mix `choice`, `type`, `order`; `order` ≤ 9 words; first word capitalised).
- `reading`: 8–12 sentences (A2: simple; B1/B2: longer, authentic-style text: email, blog, article, story); 4 questions with options in the target language.
- `sentences`: 7–8 level-appropriate sentences (≤ 12 words preferred).
- `goals`: 3–4 can-do statements in Persian.
- Every `choice`: exactly one correct option; `type`: all acceptable answers listed; reading answers answerable from the text.
- Family-friendly; no alcohol/pork. Names: Sara, Ali, Maryam, Reza, Mina, Amir + Emma, Tom, Jack, Lisa (English) / Ayşe, Mehmet, Elif, Can, Zeynep, Emre (Turkish).

| u01 | My weekend | past simple of be (was/were) | past simple regular & irregular verbs (went, saw, bought) |

| u03 | Health and the body | should / shouldn't | have to / don't have to |
| u04 | Jobs and work | present continuous vs present simple | present continuous for future arrangements |
| u05 | Comparing things | comparatives (bigger, more expensive, better) | superlatives (the biggest, the most…) |

| u07 | Shopping and money | will for offers and decisions (I'll take it) | too / enough |
