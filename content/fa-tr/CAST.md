# Cast sheet: fa-tr (Persian for Turkish speakers)

The characters are the thread of the course, so every unit must agree with this sheet. When you write a new
item, use these people in these roles. If you need someone else, such as a shop assistant or a passer-by,
leave them unnamed or use a role label. Never use "Neda" (ندا).

Spelling and `pr` are fixed. In `s` (Turkish), use the Turkish spelling in the last column.

## The Iranian family (Tehran)

One extended family. The grandparents have two daughters: Maryam's mother and Amir's mother. That makes
Maryam and Amir cousins: Amir's mother is Maryam's **xâle**, and Reza is her **şohar-e xâle** (aunt's husband).

## Unnamed roles

- u07 dialogue: the lady Emre asks for directions. Her speaker label is **Xânom** ("lady"), the word he uses.
- u08 dialogue: the shop assistant. His speaker label is **Foruşande** ("seller"), a word from the u08 reading.

## Rules for new content

1. Keep the ages: Ali 8, Sina 6, Omid 10, Mina 20, Elif 18, Emre 19. Maryam and Sara are students, Reza is a teacher, and Maryam's father is a doctor.
2. Children (Ali, Sina, Omid) go to **madrese** (school). Maryam, Sara, Mina, Ayşe and Elif are **dâneşcu** (university students).
3. Adults speak to each other in the polite form (**şomâ**). Friends and family use **to**.
4. When you change a name, change `t`, `pr` and `s` together, along with any reading question that refers to it.
