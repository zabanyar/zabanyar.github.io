# en-fa cast sheet (English for Persian speakers, A1–C1)

The same few people appear in dialogues, readings and examples at every level. Keep their facts as listed here.
Pass 5 (Nov 2026) made all levels agree with this sheet.

**Timeline.** A1 shows the family when Mina and Amir are teenagers. From A2 on, the story is set about fifteen years
later: Mina and Amir are working adults, and the friends have jobs and families. Ages are given only in A1
(and Jack's in A2 u03).

## One-off roles (not the cast)

Shop assistants, doctors, airline staff, radio hosts and people in news or feature readings get names outside the
cast, so they never clash with it: Ben (A1 u08 shop), Dr. Ahmadi (A2 u03), Kate (A2 u07 shop), Anna (B1 u07 airline),
David (B2 u01, retiring), Kaveh (B2 u02, Sara's school friend), Leila (B2 u02 reading), Ann Clark (B2 u05), Daniel Hill
(B2 u07), Karen Park and Parisa Ahmadi (B2 u10), Shirin Moradi (C1 u03), Nasrin Karimi (C1 u06), Rachel (C1 u07 radio),
Dr. Rahimi (C1 u08). Real people (Maryam Mirzakhani, Hafez) keep their names. First-person readings without a name are
anonymous narrators.

## Rules for new content

- Use only these names for the cast and never the name "Neda". A new one-off role gets a new common name that is not
  used for another role in the same or a neighboring level.
- A one-line grammar example may use a cast name for a neutral fact ("Mina has a cold.", "Tom watches TV."). It must
  not give a cast member another job, age, city, partner or family.
- Before a dialogue or reading, check the speaker's line above (job, family, city, who knows whom).
